# Variables and assignment — exercises

> Topics: variables, `=`, reassignment, `+=`, `-=`, `*=`, calculations using variable names. Use clear names for inputs and results.

### 1. Image dimensions.

An image changes from `800 × 600` to `1200 × 900` pixels. Calculate both pixel counts using the same dimension variables and the same formula.

### 2. Raster resolution.

A raster has `650` columns, `400` rows, and `10` m pixels. Calculate its ground area in km². Recalculate for `20` m pixels.

### 3. Disk usage.

A disk contains `250` GiB of data. Another `18` GiB and `12` GiB are added. Then `5` GiB are deleted. Calculate the final usage.

### 4. Equipment budget.

A laboratory has a budget of `3000` PLN. A camera costs `640` PLN, a disk `780` PLN, and a monitor `1150` PLN. Calculate the remaining budget.

### 5. Sensor calibration.

A sensor reports a height of `182.4` m. Its positive systematic error is `1.8` m. Calculate the corrected height. The estimated error is then changed to `1.2` m. Recalculate without changing the original measurement.

### 6. Travel time.

A route is `42` km long. Calculate travel time at `56` km/h and `70` km/h. How many hours are saved?

### 7. Coordinate correction.

A point has coordinates `x = 423500` m and `y = 5547900` m. Corrections are `+12.5` m for `x` and `-8` m for `y`. Calculate corrected coordinates. Change the `x` correction to `+13` m and update the result.

### 8. Sampling plan.

A team collects `32` samples per day. Calculate totals for `7` and `5` days. Then double the daily target and recalculate for `5` days.

### 9. Multiband image.

An image has `1000` columns, `800` rows, `3` bands, and `2` bytes per pixel per band. Calculate the size in MiB. Recalculate after adding a fourth band.

### 10. Stored result.

A square plot has a side length of `35` m. Store its area in a separate variable. Change the side length to `40` m. Check the stored area, then update it.
