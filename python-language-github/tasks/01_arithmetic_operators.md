# Arithmetic operators – tasks

Use only the material covered up to and including this chapter.

Use arithmetic expressions only. Do not use variables or function calls.

## Task 1. Raster coverage

A raster has 1,200 columns and 800 rows. Each square pixel covers 10 m × 10 m. Calculate the number of pixels, the total area in m² and the area in km². Use arithmetic expressions only.

## Task 2. Storage devices

Archive 1,000 files of 75 MiB each on drives with 8 GiB of usable space each (1 GiB = 1,024 MiB). Files cannot be split. Calculate how many complete files fit on one drive, the minimum number of drives needed and the total unused space in MiB.

## Task 3. Flight duration

A survey flight lasts 9,847 seconds. Express the duration in whole hours, remaining whole minutes and remaining seconds.

## Task 4. Distance in a plane

Two survey points have coordinates (120, 80) m and (150, 120) m. Calculate their straight-line distance using the square root of the sum of the squared coordinate differences. Use a power of 0.5 for the square root.

## Task 5. Raster tile index

A point is 3 m west of the grid origin. Tiles are 10 m wide; tile 0 starts at the origin and tile indices increase eastwards. Use floor division to find the tile index and modulo to find the distance from its western edge.
